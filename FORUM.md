## Putting back the EQ Freq knobs at the right position

_WBS TECHNICAL SUPPORT · Jan 26, 2011|44_

> I dont look at numbers that much when i do sound but i'm working on someone else's gear so...
> i did some testing last night with pink and white noise to see where the freq knobs on the the M460 and M463 should start and end. 
> I used some fine analyzer plugins.
> I dont know if it's a good way to do it but it sure was interesting to have a look. 
> I dont think the 6 o'clock position on my M460 is a good starting position for the Freq. 
> it looks more like the freq knob should start at around 7:30 o'clock at least for the low and high freq.
> For the mid, i think it should start a litlle bit after the 2 to be on the money.
> It was much easier to see with the high Q.
>
> anybody did some testing or found some info in manuals regarding that matter ?
